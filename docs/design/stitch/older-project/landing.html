The Operational Bottleneck

Materials shouldn’t disappear into spreadsheets.

Construction teams often rely on spreadsheets, paper records, calls, and messaging apps to manage inventory. This makes it difficult to know what is available, where materials were used, and how much was lost or wasted.

01

Poor stock visibility

Teams struggle to know exactly what is available across different sites, leading to emergency reorders and idle project labor.

02

Untracked consumption

Materials leave storage without clear records of which specific structural activity, contractor, or floor level used them.

03

Avoidable waste & theft

Damage, improper handling, weather exposure, and unaccounted site losses are discovered weeks later during monthly reconciliation.
