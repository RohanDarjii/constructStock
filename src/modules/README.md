# Capability modules

The `auth` module owns company onboarding and sessions. `organization`, `sites`, `materials`, `suppliers`, and `users` own profile, master data, and access workflows. `purchasing` and `receiving` own orders and receipts. `inventory` owns balances, batches, and the stock ledger; `consumption` and `wastage` coordinate inventory mutations through its allocation service. `alerts` owns stock threshold state, acknowledgment, and alert presentation; inventory writers call its reconciliation service inside their transactions. `dashboard` and `reports` own tenant-scoped read models and exports. `files` owns private attachment metadata, upload authorization, and cleanup. `audit` owns audit persistence and authorized history reads. As behavior is implemented, a module may contain `components/` for feature UI, `schemas/` for Zod contracts, `services/` for business workflows, `repositories/` for scoped persistence, `models/` for Mongoose schemas, and `mappers/` for API/UI representations. Avoid generating empty layers.

V1 modules: `auth`, `organization`, `users`, `sites`, `materials` (including categories), `suppliers`, `purchasing`, `receiving`, `inventory`, `consumption`, `wastage`, `transfers`, `alerts`, `dashboard`, `reports`, `files`, and `audit`.

Module services call other modules through explicit operations where practical. Critical stock workflows coordinate the business record, batch, balance, ledger, audit, and idempotency updates in one MongoDB transaction. Keep all queries scoped to the active organization, with site authorization at the service boundary.
