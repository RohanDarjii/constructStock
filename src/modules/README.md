# Capability modules

The `auth` module owns company onboarding and sessions. `sites`, `materials`, `suppliers`, and `users` own their master data and access workflows. `purchasing` and `receiving` own orders and receipts. `inventory` owns balances, batches, and the stock ledger; `consumption` and `wastage` coordinate inventory mutations through its allocation service. `alerts` owns stock threshold state, acknowledgment, and alert presentation; inventory writers call its reconciliation service inside their transactions. `files` owns private photo metadata, upload authorization, and cleanup. `audit` owns audit persistence. As behavior is implemented, a module may contain `components/` for feature UI, `schemas/` for Zod contracts, `services/` for business workflows, `repositories/` for scoped persistence, `models/` for Mongoose schemas, and `mappers/` for API/UI representations. Avoid generating empty layers.

Planned v1 modules: `auth`, `organizations`, `users`, `sites`, `materials` (including categories), `suppliers`, `purchasing`, `receiving`, `inventory`, `consumption`, `wastage`, `transfers`, `alerts`, `reports`, `files`, and `audit`.

Module services call other modules through explicit operations where practical. Critical stock workflows coordinate the business record, batch, balance, ledger, audit, and idempotency updates in one MongoDB transaction. Keep all queries scoped to the active organization, with site authorization at the service boundary.
